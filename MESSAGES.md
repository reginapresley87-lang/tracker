# Tracker: call and text wording
## Verify call (your own phone)
"Hi, this is Tracker. Your code is 4, 8, 2, 9, 1, 6. Again: 4, 8, 2, 9, 1, 6. Type it into the app. If you didn't ask for this, you can hang up."
## Verify text (fallback)
"Tracker code: 482916. Don't share this code with anyone."
## Invite call
"Hi {name}, this is Tracker. {inviter} would like to share locations with you. Nobody can see where you are unless you say yes. To answer, open the invite link we'll text you. Goodbye."
## Invite text (sent if the call isn't answered)
"{inviter} wants to share locations with you on Tracker. You won't be shown on any map unless you tap Yes, and you can stop any time. Open: {link}"
## Yes screen
"{inviter} wants to see your location. [Yes, share my location] [No thanks]. You can stop sharing any time in Settings."
## Sharing on badge
"📍 Sharing your location with {circle}. Tap to stop."
## Stopped
"You've stopped sharing. Nobody can see your location now."
